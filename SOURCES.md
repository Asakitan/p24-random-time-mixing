# Prior-result screening · 2026-11-27

- [M. Kang, Arithmetic of Bohr Frequencies Governs Uniform Mixing in Randomly Timed Quantum Spin Chains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14463), [Theorem, Outlook and supplement](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14463v1). The open classification is by modulus; the paper does not name a separate 24-site conjecture. Its positive and negative arithmetic families omit 25. We use its parity-moment target and check sufficiency directly.
- [Eight-site certificate](https://github.com/Asakitan/p8-random-time-mixing/releases/tag/proof-v1.0.1): related finite-phase simplex/interior method for q=9. Its algebraic spectrum and weights do not treat q=25.
- Searches by citation and by “uniform average mixing P24 random readout”, “randomly timed spin chains prime square”, “2609.14463 average mixing prime powers”, and “Bohr frequencies collision-free sufficiency paths” found the above path classification, but no proof specific to q=25 or a broader path theorem covering it.
- [Cao and Zhu on cycles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04207) treat a different graph and a different kind of mixing. A cycle on 25 sites is not an open chain on 24 sites.
